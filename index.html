Mission briefing

Chief engineer of a 4-person Mars outpost.

Rules

  1. Each sol you get 6 work points. Tap + and − to spend them.
  2. Systems wear down every sol. Each point restores 10%.
  3. The forecast warns of the next event.
  4. Power under 25% also drains air and food.
  5. A system under 20% hurts the crew. Health at 0 ends the mission.
  6. Start with 5 credits, earn 1 per sol. An upgrade costs 5, so pick three of four.
  7. Survive 15 sols to win.

What real engineers think about

Power feeds life support and plant growth, so one weak system drags down the others. Mission designers trade mass, power and risk against each other for every system.

Dust storms cut solar power on Mars, which is why mission planners build in power reserves. Solar flares are why crews need shielding or a storm shelter.

Curiosity’s radiation detector measured about 0.6 millisieverts of radiation per day on the Martian surface, far more than we get on Earth.
